# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple standard: be respectful, assume good faith,
and keep discussion focused on the technical substance — a client library's
behavior, a test result, a design tradeoff.

Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language aren't welcome
here. Maintainers may edit, remove, or lock issues/PRs/comments that don't
meet this standard, and may block repeat offenders.

To report a concern privately, email **itgoujie2@gmail.com**.
